Featured — Shared Research Plan

Functional and Comparative Genome Analysis of Probiotic Bacteria with Glycemic Health-Promoting Potential Shared Research Plan · MBD-SRP-01

Coordinated genome-focused evaluation of four candidate probiotic bacterial isolates from indigenous sources—integrating whole-genome assembly, safety and resistome screening, carbohydrate-active enzyme (CAZyme) profiling, and comparative genomic analysis to prioritize strains with glycemic health-promoting potential.

Standing Research Tracks

Probiotics for Human Health Research Focus · MBD-RF-01

Isolation, taxonomic identification, and multi-trait functional characterization of beneficial lactic acid and dairy-associated microbial strains, pairing in vitro gastrointestinal resilience assays with pangenome-informed evaluation.

probiotics functional-screening comparative-genomics
Microbial Natural-Product Discovery Research Focus · MBD-RF-02

Investigation of antibacterial microbial isolates, bioactive secondary metabolites, and biosynthetic gene clusters (BGCs) across environmental actinomycetes, myxobacteria, and pseudomonads to guide natural-product discovery and experimental validation.

genome-mining bioactive-metabolites bgc-analysis
Microbial Enzymes and Bioenergy Research Focus · MBD-RF-03

Optimization of extracellular proteases, keratinases, amylases, and cellulases for leather and industrial bioprocessing alongside electrogenic bacterial consortia in microbial fuel cells (MFCs) for waste-to-energy conversion.
