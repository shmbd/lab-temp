Md. Saddam Hossain is a Scientific Officer in the Microbial Biotechnology Division at the National Institute of Biotechnology (NIB), Bangladesh, holding BS and MS degrees from the University of Dhaka.

His work bridges wet-lab microbiology and dry-lab computational genomics, focusing on functional and genome-informed probiotic characterization, antimicrobial resistance (AMR) profiling, comparative microbial genomics and pangenomics, and biosynthetic gene-cluster (BGC) genome mining for bioactive secondary-metabolite discovery.
