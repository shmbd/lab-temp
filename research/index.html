Research

Our division connects indigenous microbial isolation and quantitative in vitro characterization with whole-genome sequencing, pangenomics, and biosynthetic gene-cluster (BGC) mining—bridging local microbial biodiversity in Bangladesh with human health, diagnostics, and sustainable industrial bioprocesses.

Research Directions

01 · Functional Characterization

Probiotics & Functional Health

We investigate beneficial microorganisms from indigenous dairy and fermented matrices through taxonomic identification, gastrointestinal survival and safety screening, antioxidant and antidiarrheal evaluation, and comparative genomics—carefully separating in vitro potential from demonstrated clinical effects.

  • Indigenous lactic acid bacteria isolation from local dairy and fermented sources
  • Bile-salt tolerance, antimicrobial antagonism, safety & resistome screening
  • Comparative genomics and pangenome-scale probiotic trait profiling
Representative Taxa: Weissella confusa, Enterococcus lactis, Leuconostoc lactis, Lactiplantibacillus plantarum
02 · Metabolites & Genome Mining

Microbial Drug Discovery

Our work explores antibacterial isolates and their bioactive secondary metabolites. Whole-genome sequencing and biosynthetic gene-cluster (BGC) analysis frame experimental questions while activity-guided bioassays connect genomic clusters with functional natural products.

  • Antimicrobial and antibiofilm screening of environmental & marine-associated isolates
  • In silico BGC and CAZyme repertoire mining across actinomycetes and myxobacteria
  • Druggable proteome and immunopharmacoinformatics target prioritization
Representative Taxa: Streptomyces spp., Corallococcus, Salinispora, Pseudomonas aeruginosa, Bacillus subtilis
03 · WGS & Bioinformatics

Genomics & Microbial Surveillance

Whole-genome, pangenomic, and molecular approaches support the study of microbial function, viral evolution, and antimicrobial resistance (AMR). Bioinformatics links sequence data to molecular diagnostics, electrochemical nanobiosensors, and pathogen surveillance.

  • De novo genome assembly, comparative pangenomics & virulence mapping
  • Multidrug-resistant (MDR) and ESBL pathogen surveillance in clinical & food systems
  • Viral complete-genome phylogenomics & graphene/aptamer electrochemical biosensors
Representative Targets: Citrobacter werkmanii NIB003, Salmonella, Klebsiella pneumoniae, Providencia stuartii, HBV
04 · Industrial Biotechnology

Enzymes & Sustainable Bioenergy

The division optimizes extracellular microbial biocatalysts for eco-friendly industrial bioprocessing and investigates electrogenic bacterial consortia in microbial fuel cells (MFCs) for waste-to-energy conversion and environmental remediation.

  • Bioprocess optimization & purification of alkaline proteases, keratinases, amylases & cellulases
  • Enzymatic leather dehairing and tannery/industrial effluent quality assessment
  • Electrogenic bacteria isolation & proton-exchange membrane MFC evaluation
Representative Taxa: Bacillus licheniformis, Bacillus amyloliquefaciens, Arthrobacter sp. NFH5, Aeromonas hydrophila

How Wet Lab & Dry Lab Connect

01 · Isolate

Targeted bioprospecting of bacteria from indigenous dairy foods, soils, riverine ecosystems, clinical samples, and industrial effluents across Bangladesh.

02 · Characterize

Quantitative in vitro evaluation of gastrointestinal resilience, antimicrobial activity, extracellular enzyme kinetics, and bioelectrochemical performance.

03 · Understand

Whole-genome assembly, pangenome architecture, AMR/virulence profiling, and secondary-metabolite BGC & CAZyme genome mining.
