Palash Kumar Sarker is a Senior Scientific Officer in the Microbial Biotechnology Division at the National Institute of Biotechnology (NIB), Bangladesh.

His research investigates industrial microbial enzymes (including alkaline proteases, amylases, and cellulases), molecular diagnostics, surveillance of multidrug-resistant and ESBL-producing foodborne and clinical pathogens, environmental heavy-metal assessment, and sustainable waste-to-energy bioprocesses using electrogenic bacteria in microbial fuel cells.
