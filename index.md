# Microbial Science. Human Possibility.

The **Microbial Biotechnology Division (MBD)** at the [National Institute of Biotechnology (NIB)](https://nib.gov.bd/), Bangladesh studies microorganisms to develop useful products and processes for human health, industrial innovation, and sustainable energy—connecting indigenous microbial isolation and functional characterization with whole-genome sequencing and bioinformatics.
