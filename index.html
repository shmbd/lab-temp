Microbial Science. Human Possibility.

The Microbial Biotechnology Division (MBD) at the National Institute of Biotechnology (NIB), Bangladesh studies microorganisms to develop useful products and processes for human health, industrial innovation, and sustainable energy—connecting indigenous microbial isolation and functional characterization with whole-genome sequencing and bioinformatics.

Integrated Wet & Dry Lab Research

We investigate beneficial lactic acid probiotics, bioactive microbial secondary metabolites, pathogen comparative genomics and AMR surveillance, and extracellular biocatalysts alongside microbial fuel cells for sustainable bioenergy.
