Dr. Abu Hashem serves as Principal Scientific Officer and Divisional Head of the Microbial Biotechnology Division at the National Institute of Biotechnology (NIB), Bangladesh. He earned his PhD (with distinction) in Nanobiotechnology from the University of Malaya.

His research program spans extracellular microbial enzymes for industrial bioprocessing, indigenous probiotic characterization, microbial fuel cells (MFCs) for sustainable waste-to-energy conversion, and graphene/nanomaterial-based electrochemical nucleic-acid and aptamer biosensors for clinical, food-safety, and environmental diagnostics.
